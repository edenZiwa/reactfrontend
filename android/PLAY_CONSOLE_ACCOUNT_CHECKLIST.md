# Google Play Console account checklist

Garden of Mercy Foundation should publish under an organization developer account,
provided the foundation can complete Google's organization verification.

## Before registration

- Use a Google Account controlled by the foundation, not a developer's personal-only account.
- Confirm the foundation's exact registered legal name and address.
- Confirm that the website https://gardenofmercy.org/ is live and represents the foundation.
- Check whether the foundation already has a D-U-N-S number. Search Dun & Bradstreet before
  applying for another one.
- If there is no D-U-N-S number, apply directly through Dun & Bradstreet. Google says the
  application is free and may take up to 30 days.
- Prepare an official organization phone number, organization email address, named account
  contact, and a separate public developer support email and phone number.
- Ensure the Google payments profile organization name/address match the D-U-N-S record.

## Account registration

1. Open https://play.google.com/console/signup
2. Choose an Organization account for the nonprofit foundation.
3. Complete the Developer Distribution Agreement.
4. Pay the one-time US$25 full-distribution registration fee.
5. Complete identity and contact verification requested by Google.
6. Save the account owner/recovery details securely and add a second trusted foundation
   administrator through Play Console user permissions after registration.

## Important notes

- Do not create the account as Personal merely to bypass organization verification. The
  account should be owned by the foundation.
- Google requires a D-U-N-S number for organization accounts. If the foundation cannot
  obtain one because its region is unsupported, contact Google Play support before creating
  the account and request the documented alternative verification route.
- Google may publicly display the organization's legal name/address and developer contact
  information. Use official foundation details and understand the public disclosure before
  submitting.
- Do not share passwords, payment-card details, government IDs, or private verification
  documents in this repository or in chat.
- The Android package ID `org.gardenofmercy.app` remains proposed until it is checked and
  registered in the developer console. Package IDs cannot be changed after publication.
